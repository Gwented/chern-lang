E0002: Compiler safety limit

These safety limits exist to ensure security issues are addressed.

Internal safety limits

  • Max modules = 800 | Accounting for the max region size that means at most 25 Mib can be taken
  • Max recursion limit = 1024
  • Max region size = 32 Kib

Note: None of these limits are final and could be increased (Never decreased)
